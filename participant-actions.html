1. Get your assistant ready.

2. Explore an example and keep what you learn.

Try a practice example, notice how context changes the help, and save useful notes. Open the saved file, then find it again in a fresh conversation with access to the same folder. You can leave your own project choice open.

During Session 2

3. Use AI to think it through.

Explore ideas, ask questions and examine assumptions through the session's practice.

4. Build something useful and check it.

Work through the hands-on exercise, inspect the first version, and verify what it does.

After Session 2, looking toward Sessions 3–5

5. Start with your own needs.

With those experiences in mind, consider a recurring frustration, decision or opportunity from your work. Use your assistant to explore where AI could help. Save what you want to investigate, what information or access you need, and where you may need help. Bring that into Session 3 to compare opportunities and develop your direction.
